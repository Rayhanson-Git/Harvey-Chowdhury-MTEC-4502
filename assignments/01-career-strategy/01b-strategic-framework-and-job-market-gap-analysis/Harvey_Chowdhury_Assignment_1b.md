### Skills-gap reflection

My biggest gap is understanding the code well enough to judge and maintain what I build. I can configure tools and follow the basic structure of a project, but I still need help with many coding problems. Rebuilding my portfolio gives me a reason to practice through specific tasks, such as making project previews work and understanding how the pages respond to user actions.

My OpenClaw memory work is another starting point. I noticed high token usage and poor retention between sessions. I wrote instructions, organized information in Obsidian, and used an existing repository to restructure the memories. Recall became better and token usage went down somewhat, but I did not measure the change. That experience shows me why testing matters. I want to use repeatable questions and record results instead of saying a system feels better.

I also need better documentation. Keeping explanations, failures, and improvements alongside each project would help me learn and give people something concrete to review. My goal is to become more independent while still learning from AI and from people with stronger technical experience.

This direction fits what I enjoy: experimenting, connecting ideas, and making something useful or interactive.
